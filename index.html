The quick answer

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product shortlist used to organize shopping finds. Useful entries include product links, categories, prices with currencies, measurements, seller notes, and dates checked. Use it to compare options consistently; a spreadsheet does not confirm stock, product quality, or authenticity.

03 / The shortlist method

Save fewer.
Compare better.

For your Litbuy finds spreadsheet, keep the same fields for every item. Consistent notes make differences easier to spot than a long collection of unlabelled links.

Explore CAFANS finds ↗

  1. Choose the category and intended use

    Decide whether you need an everyday sneaker, a lightweight layer, a matching set, or a small accessory. Record the purpose beside the link.

  2. Save the exact product and variant

    Note the listing address, seller name, color, size, and price currency. Similar photos can represent different variants.

  3. Compare evidence, not assumptions

    Read the size chart and material description. Where available, review recent item-specific photos and seller terms. Mark missing information as unknown.

  4. Check costs and revisit the listing

    Estimate delivery and applicable fees, then confirm the current price, availability, and return conditions before placing an order.

05 / Before checkout

Two checks worth keeping.

Look beyond the item price.

A Litbuy budget spreadsheet is more useful when it separates the product price from delivery and other costs.

Estimated total = item price + domestic delivery + service fees + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties

Keep each amount in a stated currency. Shipping estimates may change with parcel weight, dimensions, destination, and available delivery options.

Separate photos from proof.

Quality-control photos can help you inspect visible details. They do not independently establish authenticity, durability, or how a garment will fit.

  • Ask for clear, item-specific images when needed.
  • Compare measurements with an item you already own.
  • Check documentation for any authenticity claim.
  • Keep unresolved questions in your shortlist notes.

07 / Questions, answered

Before you build your list.

Practical answers for a more useful Litbuy spreadsheet.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product shortlist used to organize shopping finds. Useful entries include product links, categories, prices with currencies, measurements, seller notes, and dates checked. A spreadsheet is a research tool, not proof of availability or authenticity.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a comparison guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS so you can browse products and build your own shortlist.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the product's measurements with a garment you already own. Check the measurement unit and method, and review chest, shoulder, length, waist, and inseam where relevant. Do not rely on a letter size alone.

Do product photos prove authenticity?

No. Product photos and quality-control images can help assess visible details, but they do not independently establish authenticity. Check seller documentation and use authorized sellers or qualified authentication services when necessary.

How do I estimate the total cost?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping, and any applicable import taxes or duties. Treat shipping quotes and currency conversions as estimates until confirmed.

Is this an official Litbuy or designer brand website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS discovery guide. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès. Brand imagery is used as a visual reference, not as proof of CAFANS inventory or authenticity.
